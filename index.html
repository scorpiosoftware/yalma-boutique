A small boutique with a careful eye

Yalma is a Lebanese boutique built around one idea: every piece on our rails should be something we would happily wear ourselves. We keep collections small, restock often, and pick fabrics and cuts that flatter real bodies.

Like the lotus in our logo, we love things that are graceful without trying too hard: soft colours, easy silhouettes, and details you notice up close.

With love, the Yalma team
